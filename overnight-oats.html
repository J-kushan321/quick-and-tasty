Berry Overnight Oats

Creamy oats soaked overnight with yoghurt and honey, topped with fresh berries — breakfast ready before you even wake up.

⏱ 10 min + overnight chill🔥 ≈280 kcal per serving🍽 Serves 2📊 Easy🏷 Breakfast
4.8 · 22 ratings

Prep: 10 minutes  ·  Chill: Overnight (6+ hours)  ·  Method: No-cook, chilled

Why you will love these overnight oats

This is the easiest breakfast on the site — 10 minutes at night, zero cooking in the morning. The oats soak up the milk and yoghurt overnight and turn thick, creamy and lightly sweet. Grab a jar from the fridge, add berries, and breakfast is done.

Berry Overnight Oats — full recipe

Servings:Serves 2

Ingredients

  • For the oats
  • 1 cup rolled oats (90 g)
  • 1 cup milk (240 ml)
  • ½ cup Greek yoghurt (120 g)
  • 1 tbsp honey (or maple syrup)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • ¼ tsp cinnamon
  • To serve
  • 1 cup mixed berries (strawberries, blueberries, raspberries)
  • 1 tbsp honey, extra drizzle (optional)
  • 2 tbsp chopped nuts (optional)

Method — step by step

  1. Step 1 — Mix the base. Stir the oats, milk, yoghurt, honey, vanilla, chia seeds and cinnamon in a bowl until everything is combined.
  2. Step 2 — Divide into jars. Spoon the mixture into 2 jars or containers and seal with lids.
  3. Step 3 — Chill overnight. Refrigerate for at least 6 hours, or overnight, until thick and creamy.
  4. Step 4 — Add the berries. In the morning, top with fresh mixed berries.
  5. Step 5 — Serve. Drizzle with a little extra honey, add nuts if you like, and eat straight from the jar.

What to serve with it

These overnight oats are a full breakfast on their own, but they also go beautifully with:

  • As a grab-and-go breakfast — eat it straight from the jar on busy mornings.
  • With a coffee — the classic morning pairing.
  • Topped with peanut butter — a spoonful makes it extra filling.
  • With a smoothie — for a bigger breakfast spread.

Chef tips

  • Use rolled oats, not instant — instant oats go mushy overnight.
  • Make 2–3 jars at once — they keep in the fridge for up to 3 days.
  • If it is too thick in the morning, stir in a splash of milk.
  • Sweeten to taste — the berries add sweetness, so start with less honey.
  • Chia seeds are the secret — they thicken the oats and add fibre.

Variations

  • Peanut Butter & Banana: swap the berries for sliced banana and a spoonful of peanut butter.
  • Chocolate: stir in 1 tbsp cocoa powder and top with chocolate chips.
  • Tropical: use coconut milk and top with mango and shredded coconut.
  • Apple Cinnamon: add grated apple and extra cinnamon instead of berries.
  • Protein Boost: stir in a scoop of vanilla protein powder with the milk.

Make ahead & storage

Keep in the fridge in sealed jars for up to 3 days. The oats thicken the longer they sit — stir in a splash of milk before serving if needed. Add the berries fresh each morning so they stay bright.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to use yoghurt?
No — use all milk instead, but the yoghurt makes it creamier and adds protein.

Can I use steel-cut oats?
No — they stay hard and chewy. Rolled oats are the right ones.

Is it meant to be eaten cold?
Yes — straight from the fridge. If you prefer it warm, microwave for 30 seconds.

Can I make it dairy-free?
Yes — use oat milk or almond milk and coconut yoghurt.

Kitchen equipment you will need

  • 2 jars or containers with lids
  • A mixing bowl
  • A spoon
  • Measuring cups
  • A knife and chopping board (for the berries)

First time making overnight oats? The ratio is the whole secret — equal parts oats and milk, plus a spoonful of yoghurt, and it sets perfectly every time.
