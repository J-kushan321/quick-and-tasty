Strawberry Lemonade

Sweet strawberries and zesty lemons blended into a pink, refreshing lemonade — the perfect summer drink in 10 minutes.

⏱ 10 min🔥 ≈110 kcal per serving🍽 Serves 4📊 Easy🏷 Drinks
4.8 · 23 ratings

Prep: 10 minutes  ·  Cook: 0 minutes  ·  Method: Blended & stirred, no cooking

Why you will love this strawberry lemonade

This is the drink that tastes like summer in a glass — sweet strawberries, zesty lemons, and plenty of ice. No cooking, no fuss, just blend, stir and pour. Kids love it, and so will everyone at the barbecue.

Strawberry Lemonade — full recipe

Servings:Serves 4

Ingredients

  • For the lemonade
  • 2 cups fresh strawberries, hulled (300 g)
  • ¾ cup sugar (150 g)
  • 1 cup lemon juice, freshly squeezed (about 5 lemons, 240 ml)
  • 4 cups cold water (1 litre)
  • 1 cup ice cubes
  • To serve
  • Lemon slices
  • Fresh strawberries, halved
  • Mint leaves
  • Extra ice

Method — step by step

  1. Step 1 — Make the strawberry purée. Put the strawberries and half the sugar in a blender and blitz until completely smooth.
  2. Step 2 — Strain it smooth. Pour the purée through a fine sieve into a large jug, pressing with a spoon to get every drop.
  3. Step 3 — Add the lemon juice. Pour in the freshly squeezed lemon juice and stir it through the strawberry base.
  4. Step 4 — Sweeten and stir. Add the remaining sugar and the cold water. Stir well until the sugar has dissolved, then taste and adjust.
  5. Step 5 — Serve over ice. Fill tall glasses with ice, pour over the lemonade and garnish with lemon slices, strawberries and mint.

What to serve with it

This lemonade goes with almost any sunny-day food, but it is especially good with:

  • With grilled food — the classic barbecue pairing.
  • With brunch — pancakes, eggs and a tall glass of something pink.
  • With picnic sandwiches — pack it in a bottle and pour over ice.
  • With strawberry shortcake — strawberries twice, why not?
  • On its own — over lots of ice, with a lemon slice and mint.

Chef tips

  • Use ripe, sweet strawberries — they carry the flavour, so the sweeter the berries, the less sugar you need.
  • Roll the lemons on the counter before juicing — it loosens the juice and you get more out of each lemon.
  • Strain for a smooth drink — or skip the sieve if you like a rustic, pulpy lemonade.
  • Chill everything first — cold fruit, cold water and a cold jug make a much more refreshing drink.
  • Taste and adjust — strawberries vary in sweetness, so add sugar and lemon to your liking.

Variations

  • Sparkling: swap half the water for soda water, added just before serving.
  • Frozen Slushie: blend the finished lemonade with 2 cups of ice until slushy.
  • Mint: muddle a handful of mint leaves with the sugar for a fresh twist.
  • Watermelon: swap half the strawberries for watermelon chunks.
  • Less Sugar: use half the sugar and a spoonful of honey instead.

Make ahead & storage

Keeps in the fridge for up to 2 days in a covered jug — stir well before serving, as the strawberry settles at the bottom. Add ice only when serving, or it melts and waters the drink down. You can also freeze leftover lemonade in ice cube trays for a future batch.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use frozen strawberries?
Yes — thaw them first, then blend as usual.

Can I make it ahead?
Yes — it keeps for 2 days in the fridge. Stir before pouring.

Why is mine cloudy?
That is the strawberry pulp — strain it twice through a fine sieve for a clearer drink.

Can I use bottled lemon juice?
You can, but fresh lemons taste much better in this drink.

Kitchen equipment you will need

  • A blender
  • A fine sieve
  • A large jug
  • A citrus juicer
  • A long spoon for stirring

Taste before you serve. Strawberries vary in sweetness from batch to batch, so adjust the sugar and lemon to your liking — a little more lemon makes it brighter, a little more sugar makes it sweeter.
