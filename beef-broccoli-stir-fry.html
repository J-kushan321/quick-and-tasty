Beef & Broccoli Stir-Fry

Tender strips of beef and crisp broccoli tossed in a glossy, savoury sauce — faster than ordering takeaway, and it tastes better too.

⏱ 25 min🔥 ≈450 kcal per serving🍽 Serves 4📊 Easy🏷 Dinner
4.8 · 31 ratings

Prep: 10 minutes  ·  Cook: 15 minutes  ·  Method: Stir-fry

Why you will love this stir-fry

This is the takeaway favourite, made at home in 25 minutes. The beef stays tender thanks to a quick cornstarch marinade, the broccoli stays bright green and crisp, and the sauce is glossy, savoury and just sweet enough. One pan, no fuss, and dinner is on the table before the delivery driver would even arrive.

Beef & Broccoli Stir-Fry — full recipe

Servings:Serves 4

Ingredients

  • For the beef
  • 500 g flank steak, thinly sliced against the grain
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • For the sauce
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 tsp cornstarch
  • 3 tbsp water
  • For the stir-fry
  • 4 cups broccoli florets (about 300 g)
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • To serve
  • Steamed rice
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds

Method — step by step

  1. Step 1 — Marinate the beef. Toss the sliced beef with the soy sauce, cornstarch, sesame oil and black pepper. Leave for 10 minutes while you prep everything else.
  2. Step 2 — Mix the sauce. Whisk the soy sauce, oyster sauce, brown sugar, rice vinegar, sesame oil, cornstarch and water in a small bowl until smooth.
  3. Step 3 — Cook the broccoli. Bring a pot of water to the boil, add the broccoli and cook for 2 minutes until bright green and just tender. Drain and set aside.
  4. Step 4 — Sear the beef. Heat the oil in a large wok or frying pan over high heat until it shimmers. Add the beef in a single layer and sear for 1–2 minutes per side until browned. Remove and set aside.
  5. Step 5 — Bring it together. Lower the heat to medium, add the garlic and ginger and cook for 30 seconds. Return the beef and broccoli to the pan, pour in the sauce and toss for 1–2 minutes until glossy and thickened. Serve over steamed rice with sesame seeds and spring onions.

What to serve with it

This stir-fry is a full meal on its own, but it also goes beautifully with:

  • Steamed jasmine rice — soaks up the sauce beautifully.
  • Garlic fried rice — for a fuller takeaway-style meal.
  • With noodles — toss the stir-fry through cooked egg noodles instead of rice.
  • A simple cucumber salad — cool and crunchy next to the hot, savoury beef.

Chef tips

  • Slice the beef thinly against the grain — it stays tender instead of chewy.
  • Freeze the steak for 20 minutes first — it slices much more thinly and evenly.
  • Get the pan really hot before the beef goes in — that is what gives you the browned, caramelised edges.
  • Do not crowd the pan — sear in batches if needed, or the beef steams instead of browning.
  • Have everything ready before you start cooking — a stir-fry moves fast once the pan is hot.

Variations

  • Chicken & Broccoli: swap the beef for 500 g chicken breast, sliced thinly.
  • Spicy: add 1 tsp chilli flakes or 1 sliced red chilli with the garlic.
  • Extra Veg: add sliced carrots, snap peas or baby corn with the broccoli.
  • Low-Carb: serve over cauliflower rice instead of steamed rice.
  • Hoisin Twist: swap the oyster sauce for hoisin sauce for a sweeter glaze.

Make ahead & storage

Leftovers keep in the fridge in an airtight container for up to 3 days. Reheat gently in a pan over medium heat with a splash of water — the sauce loosens up again. The broccoli softens a little on reheating, so it is best eaten fresh if you can.

Frequently asked questions

What cut of beef is best?
Flank steak is ideal — thin slices stay tender. Sirloin or rump work too.

Can I use frozen broccoli?
Yes, thaw it first and pat it dry, or it will water down the sauce.

Why is my beef tough?
It was probably sliced too thick, or with the grain, or overcooked. Slice thin, against the grain, and sear quickly on high heat.

Can I make it ahead?
You can marinate the beef and mix the sauce up to a day ahead — keep both in the fridge. Cook just before serving.

Kitchen equipment you will need

  • A large wok or frying pan
  • A sharp knife (for slicing the beef thinly)
  • A small bowl (for the sauce)
  • A pot (for blanching the broccoli)
  • A spatula or tongs

First time stir-frying? The pan must be smoking hot before the beef goes in — that sizzle is what makes the beef brown instead of stew.
