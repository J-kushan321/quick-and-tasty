Street Corn Sweet Potato Bowl

Sweet, smoky, tangy and creamy all in one bowl — roasted sweet potato piled with charred street corn, lime crema and salty cotija.

⏱ 30 min🔥 ≈420 kcal per serving🍽 Serves 2📊 Easy🏷 Healthy
4.8 · 30 ratings

Prep: 10 minutes  ·  Cook: 20 minutes  ·  Method: Roasted in the oven

Why you will love this street corn sweet potato bowl

It is the kind of healthy food that does not feel like health food — caramelised sweet potato, smoky charred corn, a creamy lime sauce and salty cheese. Filling, fresh and ready in half an hour.

Street Corn Sweet Potato Bowl — full recipe

Servings:Serves 2

Ingredients

  • 2 large sweet potatoes, cubed
  • 2 cups corn kernels
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • For the lime sauce
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • To serve
  • 50 g cotija cheese, crumbled
  • Fresh coriander
  • Lime wedges

Method — step by step

  1. Step 1 — Roast the sweet potato. Toss the sweet potato cubes with olive oil, paprika, chilli powder and salt, then roast at 200°C for 20 minutes until golden at the edges.
  2. Step 2 — Char the corn. Cook the corn in a dry hot pan for 3–4 minutes, stirring once, until charred in spots.
  3. Step 3 — Make the lime sauce. Whisk the mayonnaise, lime juice and garlic into a creamy sauce.
  4. Step 4 — Build the bowls. Divide the sweet potato between bowls, pile on the charred corn, and drizzle with the lime sauce.
  5. Step 5 — Top and serve. Scatter over the cotija and coriander, add lime wedges, and serve warm.

What to serve with it

This bowl is a full meal, but it is lovely with:

  • With grilled chicken — for extra protein.
  • With a fried egg — the yolk makes its own sauce.
  • With tortilla chips — for scooping up the last bits.
  • With a cold drink — lime and corn love something icy.
  • On its own — it really does not need anything else.

Chef tips

  • Cut the sweet potato into even cubes so they cook at the same time.
  • Do not stir the corn too much — leave it alone so it chars.
  • Frozen corn works fine — just pat it dry first.
  • Feta works if you cannot find cotija.
  • Make the lime sauce while the potato roasts to save time.

Variations

  • Spicy: add a diced jalapeño and extra chilli powder.
  • Vegan: swap the mayo for vegan mayo and skip the cheese.
  • Grains: serve it over rice or quinoa to stretch it further.
  • Black beans: add a cup of black beans for more protein.
  • Air fryer: cook the sweet potato at 190°C for 15 minutes.

Make ahead & storage

Roast the sweet potato and char the corn up to 2 days ahead and keep them in the fridge. Reheat in a hot pan, then build the bowls fresh with the sauce and toppings.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use frozen corn?
Yes — pat it dry first so it chars instead of steaming.

What is cotija?
A salty, crumbly Mexican cheese — feta is the closest swap.

Can I make it vegan?
Yes — vegan mayo and no cheese, or a sprinkle of nutritional yeast.

Is it good cold?
It is — it makes a great packed lunch the next day.

Kitchen equipment you will need

  • A baking tray
  • Parchment paper
  • A frying pan
  • A small bowl (for the sauce)
  • A knife and chopping board

Leave the corn alone in the pan. Stirring too much stops it charring — let it sit and catch those smoky spots.
