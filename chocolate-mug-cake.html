Chocolate Mug Cake

Rich, gooey chocolate cake in a mug — mixed in 3 minutes, microwaved in 2, and eaten with a spoon straight from the mug.

⏱ 5 min🔥 ≈350 kcal per serving🍽 Serves 1📊 Easy🏷 Desserts

Prep: 3 minutes  ·  Cook: 2 minutes  ·  Method: Microwaved, one mug

Why you will love this chocolate mug cake

This is the dessert for when the craving hits and you want cake now — one mug, one spoon, five minutes, and a warm, gooey chocolate cake with a molten centre. No oven, no washing up, no waiting.

Chocolate Mug Cake — full recipe

Servings:Serves 1

Ingredients

  • For the cake
  • 4 tbsp plain flour
  • 4 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • ¼ tsp baking powder
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • ¼ tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp chocolate chips
  • To serve
  • Vanilla ice cream (optional)
  • Whipped cream (optional)
  • A dusting of cocoa powder

Method — step by step

  1. Step 1 — Mix the dry ingredients. In a microwave-safe mug, whisk together the flour, sugar, cocoa powder and baking powder until no lumps remain.
  2. Step 2 — Add the wet ingredients. Pour in the milk, oil and vanilla, then stir with a spoon until the batter is smooth and glossy.
  3. Step 3 — Stir in the chocolate chips. Fold in the chocolate chips — they melt into gooey pockets as the cake cooks.
  4. Step 4 — Microwave. Microwave on high for 1½ to 2 minutes, until the top is set but the centre is still slightly soft.
  5. Step 5 — Serve. Let it sit for 1 minute, then top with vanilla ice cream and eat straight from the mug.

What to serve with it

A mug cake is a dessert all on its own, but a little something on top makes it even better:

  • With vanilla ice cream — warm cake, cold ice cream, the perfect contrast.
  • With whipped cream — a soft cloud on top of the rich chocolate.
  • With fresh berries — raspberries or strawberries cut through the richness.
  • With a drizzle of chocolate sauce — for the true chocolate lovers.
  • With a cup of coffee — the classic late-night pairing.

Chef tips

  • Do not overcook it — pull it out while the centre is still slightly soft. That is what makes it gooey instead of dry.
  • Use a big mug — at least 300 ml, so the cake has room to rise without spilling over.
  • Microwaves vary — start with 90 seconds and add 15 seconds at a time if it needs more.
  • Room-temperature milk mixes in more smoothly than cold milk.
  • Eat it fresh — it firms up as it cools, so serve it straight away.

Variations

  • Peanut Butter: drop a spoonful of peanut butter into the centre before microwaving.
  • Nutella: swirl a spoonful of Nutella through the batter.
  • Mocha: add 1 tsp instant coffee to the dry ingredients.
  • Mint Chocolate: add a few drops of peppermint extract and a few extra chocolate chips.
  • Salted Caramel: drizzle caramel sauce over the top with a pinch of sea salt.

Make ahead & storage

Best eaten fresh, straight from the microwave. If you must keep one, cover it and refrigerate for up to 1 day, then reheat for 20 seconds in the microwave. Not suitable for freezing.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make it without egg?
Yes — this recipe has no egg at all.

Why did my mug cake overflow?
The mug was too small — use one that holds at least 300 ml, or make a smaller batch.

Can I use self-raising flour?
Yes — just leave out the baking powder.

How do I know when it is done?
The top should look set while the centre is still slightly soft — that is the gooey part.

Kitchen equipment you will need

  • A microwave-safe mug (at least 300 ml)
  • A small bowl (optional, for mixing)
  • A tablespoon and a teaspoon
  • A spoon for stirring
  • Measuring spoons

The secret is timing. Pull it out while the centre is still slightly soft — that is what makes it gooey instead of dry. A few seconds too long and it turns into a sponge.
