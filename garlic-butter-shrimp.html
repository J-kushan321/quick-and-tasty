Garlic Butter Shrimp

Juicy shrimp tossed in a rich garlic butter sauce with a squeeze of lemon — a restaurant-style dinner in 15 minutes.

⏱ 15 min🔥 ≈280 kcal per serving🍽 Serves 4📊 Easy🏷 Dinner
4.9 · 36 ratings

Prep: 5 minutes  ·  Cook: 10 minutes  ·  Method: Pan-seared, one pan

Why you will love this garlic butter shrimp

This is the dinner that tastes like a restaurant meal but cooks in one pan in 15 minutes — juicy shrimp, a rich garlic butter sauce, and bread to mop it all up. Fast enough for a weeknight, good enough for guests.

Garlic Butter Shrimp — full recipe

Servings:Serves 4

Ingredients

  • For the shrimp
  • 500 g large shrimp, peeled and deveined (about 24)
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • For the garlic butter sauce
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 5 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ tsp chilli flakes (optional)
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • To serve
  • Lemon wedges
  • Crusty bread or steamed rice

Method — step by step

  1. Step 1 — Season the shrimp. Pat the shrimp dry, then toss with salt, pepper and paprika.
  2. Step 2 — Sear the shrimp. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Sear the shrimp for 2 minutes per side until pink, then set aside.
  3. Step 3 — Make the garlic butter sauce. Melt the butter in the same pan. Add the garlic and chilli flakes and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Step 4 — Toss and finish. Return the shrimp to the pan. Add the lemon juice and zest and toss everything in the sauce for 1 minute.
  5. Step 5 — Serve. Sprinkle with parsley and serve straight away with lemon wedges and crusty bread.

What to serve with it

Garlic butter shrimp goes with almost anything, but it is especially good with:

  • With crusty bread — the best way to mop up every drop of the sauce.
  • With steamed rice — spoon the sauce right over it.
  • With garlic mashed potatoes — rich on rich, in the best way.
  • With a green salad — something fresh and crisp on the side.

Chef tips

  • Pat the shrimp dry — dry shrimp sears, wet shrimp steams.
  • Do not overcook them — 2 minutes per side is enough. They curl into a loose C when done; a tight O means overcooked.
  • Cook the garlic gently — burnt garlic turns bitter in seconds, so keep the heat medium and watch it.
  • Use large shrimp — small ones overcook before they get any colour.
  • Serve it straight away — the sauce sets as it cools, so get it to the table hot.

Variations

  • Spicy: add extra chilli flakes and a pinch of cayenne pepper.
  • Creamy: stir in a splash of cream at the end for a silky sauce.
  • Lemon Herb: add extra lemon and a spoonful of fresh thyme.
  • Honey Garlic: add 1 tbsp honey with the lemon juice for a sweet glaze.
  • Cajun: swap the paprika for Cajun seasoning.

Make ahead & storage

Best eaten fresh, straight from the pan. Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 2 days in an airtight container — reheat gently in a pan, never in the microwave, which makes the shrimp tough. Not suitable for freezing once cooked.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use frozen shrimp?
Yes — thaw them fully first, then pat them very dry.

What size shrimp should I buy?
Large ones (about 16/20 count) — they stay juicy while they sear.

Can I make it dairy-free?
Yes — use olive oil instead of butter, with a little extra garlic.

Why is my shrimp rubbery?
It was overcooked — 2 minutes per side is enough, and they keep cooking a little off the heat.

Kitchen equipment you will need

  • A large pan or skillet
  • Tongs
  • A knife and chopping board
  • A small bowl (for seasoning the shrimp)
  • A lemon squeezer (optional)

First time cooking shrimp? They cook fast — 2 minutes per side is enough. They are done when they curl into a loose C and turn pink all the way through.
