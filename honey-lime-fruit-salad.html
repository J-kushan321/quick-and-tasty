Honey Lime Fruit Salad

Juicy strawberries, mango, kiwi and blueberries tossed in a sweet honey-lime dressing — a fresh, colourful salad in 15 minutes.

⏱ 15 min🔥 ≈140 kcal per serving🍽 Serves 4📊 Easy🏷 Healthy
4.8 · 17 ratings

Prep: 15 minutes  ·  Cook: 0 minutes  ·  Method: Chopped & tossed, no cooking

Why you will love this honey lime fruit salad

This is the salad that tastes like summer in a bowl — sweet strawberries, juicy mango, tangy kiwi and plump blueberries, all glossy with a honey-lime dressing. No cooking, no fuss, just chop, toss and eat. It is the easiest healthy thing you will make all week.

Honey Lime Fruit Salad — full recipe

Servings:Serves 4

Ingredients

  • For the salad
  • 2 cups strawberries, hulled and halved (300 g)
  • 1 mango, peeled and cubed
  • 2 kiwis, peeled and sliced
  • 1 cup blueberries (150 g)
  • 1 cup grapes, halved (150 g)
  • 1 banana, sliced (optional)
  • For the honey-lime dressing
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp lime juice, freshly squeezed
  • 1 tsp lime zest
  • 1 tbsp fresh mint, chopped

Method — step by step

  1. Step 1 — Prep the fruit. Hull and halve the strawberries, peel and cube the mango, peel and slice the kiwis, and halve the grapes.
  2. Step 2 — Make the dressing. Whisk the honey, lime juice, lime zest and mint in a small bowl until smooth.
  3. Step 3 — Combine in a big bowl. Put all the fruit in a large bowl and pour the dressing over the top.
  4. Step 4 — Toss gently. Toss everything together with a big spoon until every piece is glossy with dressing.
  5. Step 5 — Chill and serve. Chill for 15 minutes if you have time, then serve in bowls with a little extra mint on top.

What to serve with it

This fruit salad goes with almost anything fresh and light, but it is especially good with:

  • With grilled chicken or fish — a sweet, fresh side for savoury mains.
  • At brunch — next to pancakes, eggs and coffee.
  • With yoghurt — spoon it over Greek yoghurt for a quick breakfast.
  • At a picnic — pack it in a container and serve chilled.
  • On its own — a light, sweet finish to any meal.

Chef tips

  • Cut the fruit roughly the same size — then every spoonful gets a bit of everything.
  • Use ripe fruit — the salad is only as sweet as the fruit in it.
  • Add the banana just before serving — it browns quickly once cut.
  • Chill it before serving — cold fruit tastes fresher and the dressing soaks in better.
  • Taste the dressing — add more honey if it is too tart, or more lime if it is too sweet.

Variations

  • Tropical: add pineapple chunks and a sprinkle of coconut flakes.
  • Berry Only: use all berries — strawberries, blueberries, raspberries and blackberries.
  • Citrus: add orange segments and a little grapefruit.
  • Yogurt Bowl: serve the salad over a bowl of Greek yoghurt with a drizzle of honey.
  • Sweet Heat: add a tiny pinch of chilli powder to the dressing for a sweet-heat kick.

Make ahead & storage

Keeps in the fridge for up to 2 days in a covered bowl — stir gently before serving, as the dressing settles at the bottom. Add the banana just before serving so it does not brown. Not suitable for freezing.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use frozen fruit?
Fresh is best here — thawed berries go soft and watery.

Can I make it ahead?
Yes — up to 1 day ahead in the fridge. Add the banana just before serving.

What if I do not have limes?
Lemons work too — the dressing will be a little sharper, so add a touch more honey.

Is it sweet enough without honey?
The fruit is sweet on its own — add the honey to taste, a spoonful at a time.

Kitchen equipment you will need

  • A big mixing bowl
  • A small bowl and a whisk (for the dressing)
  • A knife and chopping board
  • A citrus juicer
