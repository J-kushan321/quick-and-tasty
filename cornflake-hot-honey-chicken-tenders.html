Cornflake Hot Honey Chicken Tenders

These are the tenders all over TikTok right now — shatteringly crispy cornflake crumbs, juicy chicken inside, and a sticky hot honey drizzle that makes them impossible to stop eating.

⏱ 50 min🔥 ≈520 kcal per serving🍽 Serves 4📊 Easy🏷 Dinner
4.9 · 33 ratings

Prep: 30 minutes  ·  Cook: 20 minutes  ·  Method: Baked in the oven, no frying

Why you will love these cornflake hot honey chicken tenders

Three things make these tenders special — the cornflake crumbs bake up crunchier than any breadcrumb, the chicken stays juicy inside, and the hot honey adds a sweet heat that keeps you reaching back for one more. They are baked, not fried, and the whole tray is ready in under an hour.

Cornflake Hot Honey Chicken Tenders — full recipe

Servings:Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken tenderloins (or chicken breast cut into strips)
  • 2 cups cornflakes, crushed
  • 1 cup plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • For the hot honey
  • ¼ cup honey
  • 1 tbsp hot sauce (Frank's or sriracha)
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • To serve
  • Ranch dip
  • Extra hot honey

Method — step by step

  1. Step 1 — Crush the cornflakes. Tip the cornflakes into a bag and crush them with a rolling pin until coarse crumbs — not dust. Tip into a bowl and mix with the flour, garlic powder, paprika, salt and pepper.
  2. Step 2 — Coat the chicken. Dip each tenderloin in the beaten egg, then press it into the cornflake crumbs until fully coated. Lay the coated tenders on a lined baking tray.
  3. Step 3 — Bake until golden. Bake at 200°C for 20 minutes, flipping the tenders halfway, until golden and crisp and the chicken is cooked through.
  4. Step 4 — Make the hot honey. Warm the honey, hot sauce, chilli flakes and butter in a small pan over low heat until runny and glossy.
  5. Step 5 — Drizzle and serve. Drizzle the hot honey over the hot tenders and serve right away with ranch for dipping.

What to serve with it

These tenders are a meal on their own, but they are even better with:

  • With ranch — the cool, creamy dip for the sweet heat.
  • With coleslaw — crunchy, fresh and cooling.
  • With fries — the full comfort-food plate.
  • With corn on the cob — buttery and sweet beside the tenders.
  • On their own — straight from the tray, while they are still hot.

Chef tips

  • Crush the cornflakes coarse, not to dust — big crumbs are what make them shatteringly crispy.
  • Use a wire rack on the tray if you have one — the air underneath keeps the bottoms crisp.
  • Do not crowd the tray — give every tender space, or use two trays.
  • Flip them halfway so both sides go golden.
  • Drizzle the hot honey while the tenders are still hot — it sticks much better.

Variations

  • Spicy: add an extra teaspoon of chilli flakes and a hotter sauce.
  • Air fryer: cook at 190°C for 12–14 minutes, flipping halfway.
  • Buffalo: swap the hot honey for buffalo sauce and serve with blue cheese dip.
  • Parmesan: mix ¼ cup grated Parmesan into the cornflake crumbs.
  • Gluten-free: use gluten-free cornflakes and gluten-free flour.

Make ahead & storage

Coat the tenders up to 4 hours ahead and keep them in the fridge, then bake fresh. Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 2 days — reheat in a hot oven or air fryer to bring back the crispiness. Never microwave them — they go soft.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use chicken breast?
Yes — cut it into even strips about the width of two fingers, and add 2–3 minutes to the bake.

Can I make it less spicy?
Yes — use half the hot sauce and skip the chilli flakes; the honey is sweet on its own.

Can I make them in an air fryer?
Yes — 190°C for 12–14 minutes, flipping halfway. Work in batches so they stay crisp.

How do I keep them crispy?
Bake on a wire rack, do not cover them, and drizzle the honey right before serving.

Kitchen equipment you will need

  • A baking tray
  • Parchment paper
  • A wire rack (optional)
  • Three shallow bowls
  • A small pan (for the hot honey)
