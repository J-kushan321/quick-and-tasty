Iced Matcha Latte

Earthy green matcha whisked smooth and poured over cold milk and ice — a calm, creamy café drink in 5 minutes.

⏱ 5 min🔥 ≈120 kcal per serving🍽 Serves 1📊 Easy🏷 Drinks

Why you will love this iced matcha latte

This is the calm version of an iced coffee — smooth, earthy matcha layered over cold milk, with a gentle lift instead of a jolt. It takes 5 minutes, needs no machine, and looks like it came from a café.

Iced Matcha Latte — full recipe

Servings:Serves 1

Ingredients

  • For the latte
  • 1 tsp matcha powder
  • 2 tbsp hot water (30 ml)
  • 1 cup cold milk (240 ml)
  • 1 cup ice cubes
  • 1–2 tsp honey (optional)
  • To serve
  • Extra ice
  • A dusting of matcha (optional)

Method — step by step

  1. Step 1 — Whisk the matcha. Sift the matcha into a small bowl, add the hot water, and whisk until smooth and frothy with no lumps.
  2. Step 2 — Fill the glass with ice. Fill a tall glass right up to the top with ice cubes.
  3. Step 3 — Pour the milk. Pour the cold milk over the ice, leaving a little room at the top.
  4. Step 4 — Add the matcha. Stir the honey into the whisked matcha if you like it sweet, then pour it slowly over the milk so the layers show.
  5. Step 5 — Stir and serve. Give it a gentle stir with a straw, dust a little matcha on top, and serve straight away.

What to serve with it

This matcha latte is a drink on its own, but it is especially good with:

  • With toast — a light breakfast with something to dunk.
  • With a pastry — a croissant or a muffin on the side.
  • With fruit — berries or a banana for a fresh snack.
  • After lunch — a calm afternoon lift without the coffee jitters.
  • On its own — cold, creamy and quietly energising.

Chef tips

  • Sift the matcha first — it clumps easily, and lumps never whisk out.
  • Use water that is hot, not boiling — boiling water makes matcha bitter.
  • Pour the matcha slowly over the back of a spoon — that is how you get the pretty layers.
  • Use cold milk straight from the fridge — warm milk melts the ice and waters it down.
  • Sweeten to taste — matcha is earthy, so a little honey goes a long way.

Variations

  • Oat Milk: swap the milk for oat milk — it is extra creamy with matcha.
  • Vanilla: add a drop of vanilla extract to the milk.
  • Coconut: use coconut milk for a tropical twist.
  • Iced Matcha Lemonade: swap the milk for lemonade and skip the honey.
  • Protein: blend a scoop of vanilla protein powder into the milk.

Make ahead & storage

Best made fresh — the ice melts and the layers disappear within 20 minutes. You can whisk the matcha ahead and keep it in the fridge for a day, then pour it over fresh ice and milk when you want it.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of matcha should I use?
Ceremonial grade tastes smoothest, but any good-quality culinary matcha works for a latte.

Why is my matcha lumpy?
It was not sifted — push it through a small sieve before whisking.

Can I make it hot instead?
Yes — whisk the matcha with hot water, then top up with steamed milk.

Does matcha have caffeine?
Yes, about half as much as coffee — but it feels calmer and lasts longer.

Kitchen equipment you will need

  • A small bowl
  • A whisk (a bamboo whisk is traditional, a small metal one works too)
  • A tall glass
  • A small sieve
  • A spoon

Sift the matcha first. Matcha clumps the moment it meets water — a quick sift is the difference between a smooth latte and a lumpy one.
