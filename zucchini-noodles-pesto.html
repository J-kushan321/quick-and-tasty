Zucchini Noodles with Pesto

Light zucchini noodles tossed in fresh basil pesto with sweet cherry tomatoes and Parmesan — a fresh, low-carb dinner in 20 minutes.

⏱ 20 min🔥 ≈280 kcal per serving🍽 Serves 2📊 Easy🏷 Healthy
4.9 · 27 ratings

Prep: 10 minutes  ·  Cook: 10 minutes  ·  Method: Sautéed & tossed

Why you will love these zucchini noodles

This is the pasta night that leaves you feeling light — fresh zucchini noodles, barely cooked so they stay a little crisp, tossed in bright homemade pesto with sweet cherry tomatoes and salty Parmesan. All the flavour of pesto pasta, none of the heaviness.

Zucchini Noodles with Pesto — full recipe

Servings:Serves 2

Ingredients

  • For the pesto
  • 2 cups fresh basil leaves
  • ¼ cup pine nuts
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • ⅓ cup olive oil
  • ½ tsp salt
  • For the noodles
  • 4 medium zucchini, spiralized (about 600 g)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • Extra Parmesan, to serve

Method — step by step

  1. Step 1 — Spiralize the zucchini. Spiralize the zucchini into noodles and pat them dry with a clean towel.
  2. Step 2 — Make the pesto. Blend the basil, pine nuts, Parmesan, garlic, olive oil and salt in a food processor until smooth, scraping down the sides once.
  3. Step 3 — Sauté the noodles. Heat the olive oil in a big pan over medium-high heat and toss the zucchini noodles for 2 minutes — just until warm, not soft.
  4. Step 4 — Toss with pesto. Take the pan off the heat, add the pesto and half the tomatoes, and toss until every noodle is coated.
  5. Step 5 — Serve with tomatoes. Serve in bowls topped with the rest of the tomatoes and extra Parmesan.

What to serve with it

These noodles are a light dinner on their own, but they are especially good with:

  • With grilled chicken — sliced on top for a bigger meal.
  • With garlic bread — for mopping up the extra pesto.
  • With a salad — a simple green salad on the side.
  • With shrimp — a few garlicky shrimp tossed through.
  • On its own — fresh, green and complete.

Chef tips

  • Do not overcook the noodles — 2 minutes is enough, or they go soggy.
  • Pat the noodles dry — wet noodles water down the pesto.
  • Toss the pesto off the heat — hot pesto turns dark and bitter.
  • Use fresh basil — wilted basil makes a dull, brown pesto.
  • Toast the pine nuts — 2 minutes in a dry pan makes the pesto taste richer.

Variations

  • Sun-Dried Tomato: blend sun-dried tomatoes into the pesto for a red version.
  • Creamy: stir 2 tbsp cream cheese into the finished noodles.
  • Spicy: add a pinch of chilli flakes to the pesto.
  • Walnut: swap the pine nuts for walnuts.
  • With Pasta: toss half zucchini noodles and half spaghetti for a heartier bowl.

Make ahead & storage

Best eaten fresh — the noodles soften as they sit. The pesto keeps in the fridge for up to 1 week in a jar with a thin layer of olive oil on top. Leftovers keep for 1 day in the fridge; eat them cold or warm them very gently.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use store-bought pesto?
Yes — use about ½ cup of good-quality pesto.

What if I do not have a spiralizer?
Use a vegetable peeler to make wide ribbons, or buy ready-made zucchini noodles.

Why are my noodles watery?
They were cooked too long, or not patted dry. Two minutes in a hot pan is all they need.

Can I add protein?
Yes — grilled chicken, shrimp or a fried egg all work beautifully.

Kitchen equipment you will need

  • A spiralizer (or a vegetable peeler)
  • A food processor
  • A large frying pan
  • A knife and chopping board
  • Tongs

Do not overcook the noodles. Two minutes in a hot pan is enough — the noodles should stay a little crisp, like al dente pasta, not soft like cooked vegetables.
