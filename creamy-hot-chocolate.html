Creamy Hot Chocolate

Rich, silky hot chocolate made with real chocolate and warm milk — a cosy hug in a mug in 10 minutes.

⏱ 10 min🔥 ≈280 kcal per serving🍽 Serves 2📊 Easy🏷 Drinks
4.8 · 26 ratings

Prep: 5 minutes  ·  Cook: 5 minutes  ·  Method: Heated & whisked

Why you will love this creamy hot chocolate

This is not the thin, watery hot chocolate from a packet — it is rich, silky and made with real chocolate, whisked into warm milk until it is thick enough to coat a spoon. Ten minutes, one pan, and the whole house smells like chocolate.

Creamy Hot Chocolate — full recipe

Servings:Serves 2

Ingredients

  • For the hot chocolate
  • 2 cups milk (500 ml)
  • 100 g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp sugar (or to taste)
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • To serve
  • Whipped cream
  • Chocolate shavings
  • Marshmallows (optional)

Method — step by step

  1. Step 1 — Heat the milk. Pour the milk into a saucepan and heat over medium heat until warm and steaming — do not let it boil.
  2. Step 2 — Add the chocolate. Take the pan off the heat and add the chopped dark chocolate and cocoa powder.
  3. Step 3 — Whisk until smooth. Whisk until the chocolate is fully melted and the drink is smooth and creamy. Stir in the sugar, vanilla and salt.
  4. Step 4 — Sweeten to taste. Taste and add a little more sugar if you like it sweeter, or a splash more milk if it is too rich.
  5. Step 5 — Pour and top. Pour into mugs and top with whipped cream, chocolate shavings or marshmallows. Serve straight away.

What to serve with it

A mug of this is perfect on its own, but try it with:

  • With whipped cream — piled high with chocolate shavings.
  • With marshmallows — the classic, melting into the top.
  • With biscuits — for dunking, obviously.
  • With cinnamon — a pinch on top for a warm spice.
  • With a book — and nowhere to be for a while.

Chef tips

  • Real chocolate, not just cocoa, is what makes it silky — use at least 70% dark.
  • Do not let the milk boil — warm and steaming is enough.
  • Whisk constantly while the chocolate melts so it never catches.
  • Add a pinch of salt — it makes the chocolate taste more chocolatey.
  • Warm the mugs first with hot water so the drink stays hot longer.

Variations

  • Mint: stir in a drop of peppermint extract and top with a candy cane.
  • Spiced: add a pinch of cinnamon and a tiny pinch of chilli.
  • Orange: add a strip of orange zest while heating the milk.
  • White chocolate: swap the dark chocolate for white chocolate and vanilla.
  • Mocha: add a shot of espresso for a coffee-chocolate hug.

Make ahead & storage

Best made fresh and drunk straight away. You can make a double batch and keep it in the fridge for up to 2 days — reheat gently on the stove, whisking well, and add the toppings fresh.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use milk chocolate?
Yes — it will be sweeter, so cut the sugar to 1 tablespoon.

Can I make it dairy free?
Yes — use oat milk, it is the creamiest dairy-free option.

Why is mine grainy?
The chocolate probably was not fully melted — keep whisking over low heat.

Can I make it in the microwave?
Yes — heat the milk in a jug, then whisk in the chocolate.

Kitchen equipment you will need

  • A small saucepan
  • A whisk
  • Two mugs
  • A chopping board and knife

Real chocolate is the whole secret. A bar of good dark chocolate, melted into warm milk, is what makes it silky — cocoa powder alone never tastes the same.
