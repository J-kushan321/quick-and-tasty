Buffalo Chicken Wings

Crispy oven-baked wings tossed in tangy buffalo sauce — sticky, spicy and impossible to stop eating.

⏱ 45 min🔥 ≈420 kcal per serving🍽 Serves 4📊 Easy🏷 Snacks
4.9 · 35 ratings

Prep: 10 minutes  ·  Cook: 35 minutes  ·  Method: Baked & tossed in sauce

Why you will love these buffalo chicken wings

These are the wings that vanish at every gathering — oven-baked until the skin goes shatteringly crisp, then tossed in a buttery, tangy buffalo sauce that coats every wing. No deep fryer, no mess, just a tray and a bowl.

Buffalo Chicken Wings — full recipe

Servings:Serves 4

Ingredients

  • For the wings
  • 1 kg chicken wings
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • For the buffalo sauce
  • ⅓ cup hot sauce (80 ml)
  • 3 tbsp butter, melted
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp vinegar
  • To serve
  • Celery sticks
  • Blue cheese dip
  • Extra sauce

Method — step by step

  1. Step 1 — Season the wings. Pat the wings dry, then toss them with olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder and paprika.
  2. Step 2 — Bake until crisp. Spread the wings in a single layer on a lined tray and bake at 200°C for 35 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and crisp.
  3. Step 3 — Toss in sauce. Whisk the hot sauce, melted butter, honey and vinegar together, then toss the hot wings in the sauce until every wing is coated.
  4. Step 4 — Rest a minute. Let the wings sit for 1 minute so the sauce sticks instead of sliding off.
  5. Step 5 — Serve with celery. Pile the wings onto a plate with celery sticks and blue cheese dip.

What to serve with it

These wings are a snack on their own, but they are especially good with:

  • With celery & dip — the classic cool crunch next to the heat.
  • With fries — a proper game-day plate.
  • With a cold drink — beer, soda or lemonade.
  • With ranch — if blue cheese is not your thing.
  • On their own — with plenty of napkins.

Chef tips

  • Pat the wings very dry — wet skin never goes crisp.
  • Give them space on the tray — crowded wings steam instead of crisping.
  • Flip them halfway — both sides need the heat.
  • Toss the wings while they are hot — the sauce sticks much better.
  • Taste the sauce first — add more honey if it is too hot, more hot sauce if it is too mild.

Variations

  • Extra Hot: add a pinch of cayenne to the sauce.
  • Honey BBQ: swap the hot sauce for barbecue sauce.
  • Garlic Parmesan: toss the baked wings in garlic butter and Parmesan instead.
  • Air Fryer: air fry at 200°C for 20 minutes, shaking halfway.
  • Boneless: use chicken breast chunks, breaded and baked, then tossed in sauce.

Make ahead & storage

Best eaten hot and saucy. Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 3 days — reheat them in a hot oven for 10 minutes to bring back the crispiness, then toss with a little extra sauce. Not suitable for freezing once sauced.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use frozen wings?
Yes — thaw them fully first and pat them very dry.

Why are my wings not crispy?
The tray was crowded, or the wings were wet. Dry them well and give them space.

How spicy are they?
Medium — the butter and honey take the edge off the hot sauce. Add more honey to cool it down.

Can I make the sauce ahead?
Yes — it keeps in the fridge for up to 1 week. Warm it gently before tossing.

Kitchen equipment you will need

  • A baking tray
  • Parchment paper
  • A big mixing bowl
  • A small bowl and whisk (for the sauce)
  • Tongs

Pat the wings very dry. Wet skin steams in the oven and never goes crisp — a good pat with paper towels is the whole secret.
