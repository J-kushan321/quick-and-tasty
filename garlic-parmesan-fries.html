Garlic Parmesan Fries

Crispy oven-baked fries tossed in garlic butter and Parmesan — golden, cheesy and dangerously easy to finish in one sitting.

⏱ 35 min🔥 ≈310 kcal per serving🍽 Serves 4📊 Easy🏷 Snacks
4.8 · 30 ratings

Prep: 10 minutes  ·  Cook: 25 minutes  ·  Method: Baked in the oven, no frying

Why you will love these garlic parmesan fries

These are the fries everyone fights over — crispy on the outside, fluffy inside, and coated in warm garlic butter with salty Parmesan and fresh parsley. They bake in the oven, so there is no deep frying and no greasy mess — just one tray and a bowl.

Garlic Parmesan Fries — full recipe

Servings:Serves 4

Ingredients

  • For the fries
  • 4 large russet potatoes (about 800 g), cut into fries
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • For the garlic Parmesan topping
  • 3 tbsp butter, melted
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • To serve
  • Ketchup or garlic mayo
  • Extra Parmesan

Method — step by step

  1. Step 1 — Cut and soak the potatoes. Cut the potatoes into even fries, then soak them in cold water for 30 minutes. Drain and pat them very dry.
  2. Step 2 — Season and spread on a tray. Toss the fries with olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder and paprika, then spread them in a single layer on a lined baking tray.
  3. Step 3 — Bake until golden. Bake at 220°C for 25 minutes, flipping the fries halfway, until golden and crisp at the edges.
  4. Step 4 — Make the garlic butter. While the fries bake, melt the butter in a small pan and stir in the minced garlic for 30 seconds — do not let it brown.
  5. Step 5 — Toss and serve. Tip the hot fries into a big bowl, pour over the garlic butter, add the Parmesan and parsley, and toss until every fry is coated. Serve right away.

What to serve with it

These fries go with anything you would normally eat with fries — but they are especially good with:

  • With burgers — the classic pairing, only better.
  • With fried chicken — crispy on crispy, with a cold drink.
  • With sandwiches — a cheesy, garlicky side for a simple sandwich.
  • With a dip — ketchup, garlic mayo or ranch.
  • On their own — straight from the bowl, while they are still hot.

Chef tips

  • Soak the fries in cold water for 30 minutes — it washes off the starch and makes them much crispier.
  • Pat them very dry before seasoning — wet fries steam instead of crisping.
  • Do not crowd the tray — give every fry space, or use two trays.
  • Flip them halfway through baking so both sides go golden.
  • Add the garlic butter and Parmesan while the fries are still hot — it melts and sticks much better.

Variations

  • Spicy: add 1 tsp chilli flakes or a pinch of cayenne to the seasoning.
  • Truffle: swap the olive oil for truffle oil and finish with extra Parmesan.
  • Cheese Fries: pour warm cheese sauce over the finished fries.
  • Cajun: season with Cajun spice mix instead of garlic powder and paprika.
  • Rosemary: add fresh rosemary to the tray for the last 10 minutes of baking.

Make ahead & storage

Best eaten fresh and hot, straight from the oven. Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 2 days — reheat them in a hot oven or air fryer to bring back the crispiness. Never microwave them — they go soft.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use frozen fries?
Yes — bake them straight from frozen following the packet time, then toss with the garlic butter and Parmesan.

Why did my fries go soggy?
Usually a crowded tray, or skipping the soak and the drying step. Give them space and keep them dry.

Can I make them in an air fryer?
Yes — 200°C for 15–18 minutes, shaking the basket halfway. Work in batches so they stay crisp.

Can I use sweet potatoes?
Yes — they cook a little faster, so check them after 20 minutes.

Kitchen equipment you will need

  • A baking tray
  • Parchment paper
  • A big mixing bowl
  • A knife and chopping board
  • A small pan (for the garlic butter)

Soaking the fries in cold water is the crispiness secret. It washes the starch off the outside, so the fries bake up golden and crisp instead of soft and steamy.
